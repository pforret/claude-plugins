# busy --all prints nothing at all when no repo has busy or plan items

## Context
With the output now captured in busy_cmd, an empty `busy --all` result would print a lone empty line; before, busy_all simply printed nothing.

## Options considered
- Always `echo "$out"`: a stray blank line.
- `[ -z "$out" ] || echo "$out"`: unchanged behaviour (nothing printed), what was done.

## Decision
busy --all prints nothing at all when no repo has busy or plan items

## Consequences
`busy --all` with no repos having busy or plan items prints nothing and no legend; the busy skill then says nothing is in progress.
