# fodo pick also lists busy items and resumes them with /fodo:start B<n>

## Context
The request asked for backlog and planned items only. Busy items (`B<n>`), including waiting ones, are the other thing you would pick to work on.

## Options considered
- Backlog + plan only: matches the request, smallest list.
- Also busy items: picking one runs `/fodo:start B<n>`, which resumes a waiting item; slightly larger scope.

## Decision
fodo pick also lists busy items and resumes them with /fodo:start B<n>

## Consequences
The picker lists three tables in order: backlog `n`, plan `P<n>`, busy `B<n>`. Waiting busy items show their reason. Only backlog items get the plan/start question; the others go straight to `/fodo:start`.
